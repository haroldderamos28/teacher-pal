1. What TeachPaL is

TeachPaL is an English speaking coach. It listens to speech, turns it into text and uses AI to suggest corrections, more natural phrasing, alternatives and feedback. On Tier 2 and Premium you can also type your thoughts in the "Write your thoughts" box and get the same kind of refinement.

You can use TeachPaL in two ways:

  • As a trainer or teacher, while you teach someone else (for example, in an online English lesson); or
  • As a learner, to practise your own speaking: for job interviews, work, presentations, English tests or everyday conversation.

TeachPaL is not a substitute for your own judgement or for a qualified teacher. Speech recognition can mishear words, and AI suggestions can be wrong, incomplete or unsuitable. Check anything before you rely on it or share it with anyone. TeachPaL does not score pronunciation, predict or guarantee test results, or guarantee any job, grade or other outcome.
