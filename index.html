      Get going in 4 steps
      1. Get blocks. On the Dashboard, open Buy blocks, pick a block type and size, pay by QR and send us the reference number. Your blocks show up once the payment is checked. New? Start with the 3-block Try it pack.
      2. Set up your class. On the Live class tab, choose the block type, then the Mode, Level and Support language that fit your student. You can change these mid-class too.
      3. Press Start Listening. Your browser asks for permission once. For an online class, choose Tab / window audio, pick the tab or window your class is in, and tick Share audio. For speaking practice on your own, use Microphone.
      4. Teach as usual. As the student speaks, each sentence gets a card: what was heard, the correction, a more natural way to say it, and more. Press Pause between students, Stop when the class ends, and Start fresh for the next one.
      What the settings do
      Mode

      How the AI rewrites what the student said. Natural sounds native; Casual is everyday talk; Business and Professional suit workplace English; Concise is shorter; Student-friendly is the simplest wording; Kids is simple and cheerful, with no grammar terms. You can switch mid-class.

      Level

      The student's English level. It keeps suggestions at the right difficulty and skips very short replies (under 4 words for Basic, 6 for Intermediate, 8 for Advanced).

      Support language

      Choose a language and beginners also see a translation of the correction. Available on Tier 2 and Premium.

      Audio source

      Tab / window audio listens to your online lesson and skips your own voice. Microphone is for practising on your own. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer for tab audio.

      How blocks are used
      • 1 block covers up to 30 minutes, so a 25-minute class uses 1 block.
      • The first 3 minutes of a class are free. A class under 3 minutes costs nothing.
      • Time while you're paused isn't counted, so pause between students.
      • Your blocks are on the Dashboard, with your class and purchase history.
      Tips and extras
      • Write your thoughts (Tier 2 and Premium): type a sentence or short paragraph, press send, and get it refined. Handy for speaking-test prep and when you'd rather type than talk.
      • Your voice is skipped automatically with tab audio: TeachPaL uses your mic only to notice when you're talking, and ignores the lesson audio while you speak, so your own sentences (or their echo from the student's side) don't get corrected. Allow the microphone when asked, and use a headset.
      • Reading back? The list stays where you scrolled while new sentences come in. A ↑ New corrections button appears to jump to the latest.
      • Copy buttons on each card let you paste a correction into your class chat.
      • Save your defaults on the Dashboard so every class starts with your usual Mode, Level and language.
      • Teaching someone? Let your student know an AI helper is listening. TeachPaL doesn't record audio or save transcripts.
      Something not working?
      • Your own sentences are being corrected: check that you're using Tab / window audio and that you allowed the microphone (the pill next to the timer should say "Skipping your voice").
      • No sound picked up (tab audio): run the share again and make sure Share audio is ticked in the share window.
      • Microphone blocked: click the lock icon next to the web address, allow the microphone, and reload.
      • Blocks haven't appeared after paying: check the reference number you sent, then message us with it.
      • Still stuck? Use the form below, or email us, and tell us what you saw.

      Before you start with TeachPaL

      TeachPaL listens to speech, so please read and tick each point. They protect you, anyone you teach, and TeachPaL.

      🔒 TeachPaL does not record audio or save transcripts. Speech is turned into text live, and corrections stay on your screen only.
      What's new:
      • TeachPaL is now for learners practising their own English too, not only trainers.
      • New "Write your thoughts" box (Tier 2 and Premium): typed text is refined like speech and isn't stored.
      • Accounts are for adults; under 18s may use a parent's or guardian's account with permission.
      • Don't use TeachPaL during exams or interviews where outside help isn't allowed.
