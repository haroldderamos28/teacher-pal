Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

TeachPaL is a live English speaking coach. Trainers use it while teaching online lessons, and learners use it to practise their own speaking. TeachPaL listens to speech (or reads text typed in "Write your thoughts"), turns it into text and shows corrections and suggestions on the user's screen. This policy explains what information TeachPaL handles, why, and the choices you have. It is written to follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

The short version
  • We don't record lessons. Audio is streamed for live transcription and is not saved by TeachPaL.
  • We don't keep transcripts or corrections on our servers. They stay on the trainer's screen until the page is closed or cleared.
  • We store only what we need to run accounts and billing: your email, your block balances, class times and lengths, purchases and your saved settings.
  • We never sell personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

1. Who we are

TeachPaL is operated by Mark Harold De Ramos, Camaligan, Camarines Sur, Philippines ("TeachPaL", "we", "us"). For anything about your privacy, contact us at haroldderamos28@gmail.com.

2. Whose information this covers

Users are the people who sign up and use TeachPaL: trainers who use it while teaching, and learners who use it to practise their own English. Students are the people a trainer is teaching. Students don't create accounts; their speech is processed only while their trainer is using TeachPaL in a lesson.

5. Trainers' responsibilities toward students

Trainers decide when to use TeachPaL in their lessons. Before using it, trainers agree in the app to:

  • make sure using an AI listening tool is allowed by the teaching platform or school they work for;
  • let students know an AI helper is listening to give feedback, and get their consent where required;
  • not use TeachPaL with students under 18 unless a parent or guardian has agreed; and
  • not use TeachPaL to record, store or share students' personal information.

6. Security

Connections to TeachPaL are encrypted (HTTPS). Account data is protected by sign-in and database access rules, so each trainer can only see their own records. Our secret keys are kept on the server and never sent to browsers. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users and the National Privacy Commission of any personal data breach as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access, to correct, to object, to erasure or blocking, to data portability, and to be compensated for damages. You can:

  • ask for a copy of your data or ask us to correct it;
  • ask us to delete your account and data (we may keep purchase records we're required to keep for tax purposes); or
  • withdraw consent by deleting your account.

Email haroldderamos28@gmail.com and we'll reply within 15 working days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the National Privacy Commission.

8. Cookies and local storage

TeachPaL doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores your sign-in session and small preferences (like which tab you last used and how you sized the panels) so the app works properly.

9. Children

TeachPaL accounts are for adults (18 and older). A learner under 18 may use TeachPaL only through an account held by their parent or guardian, with that adult's permission and supervision. Students under 18 should only be part of a lesson that uses TeachPaL with a parent's or guardian's agreement. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we'll close it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date above and ask users to review and accept the new version in the app before their next session.
